Hydrostatic plate lab

Place a flat plate in a fluid, tilt and spin it, and see the pressure, the total force and where it acts.

Inputs

Spin 0° puts the length along the slope; spin 90° puts the width along it. A negative depth lifts the top edge above the water.

Arrows show pressure on the marked face. centroid center of pressure. ⊗ is an arrow pointing away from you, ⊙ points toward you.

Light to dark means low to high pressure. Grey is above the water.
